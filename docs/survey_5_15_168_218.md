## Deferred backlog (candidates needing larger rebases)

These are genuine feature/perf commits but were deferred because their
hunks chain on AOSP-drifted contexts (rmqueue region vendor deltas, NOHZ
helpers) or because their value is narrow for a phone GKI:

1. **Gorman reserve-semantics deep water** (.171–.194): ca8527f25736
   (rmqueue_buddy split), c1b8856c5a7d, 17dedfd6de69, 85f58ee33c6c,
   4c4e238d3ada, 735457683e23 (ALLOC_HIGHATOMIC/OOM reserve semantics).
   Requires hand-anchoring against the AOSP rmqueue() region; the
   self-consistent rename + RT pair already landed as
   `pagealloc_min_reserve_semantics`.
2. **sched/fair dst-group stats** (.212): d99f14f8b142 — find_idlest path,
   needs fair.c anchor rework around AOSP vendor hooks.
3. **sched steal-time catch-up cap** (.179): 56135262c1f9 — near-zero value
   on non-virtualized phones.
4. **sched u64 bandwidth ratios** (.210): 64d9b734b6fe — fix-flavored.
5. **blk-mq quiesced elevator switch** (.209): 9646443f28f3 — overlaps
   elevator paths the ABI suite rewrites.
6. **newidle_balance rename** (.196), iov_iter initializer renames (.210),
   inode sysctls file move (.179): churn without user-visible value.
